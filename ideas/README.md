# The module catalog

Every family a genome chooses from (`src/speedrunning_plms/strategies/`, one module each), its variants, and the
evidence for each on the 10-minute AFDB v6 speedrun; then the ideas not yet a module, and the four literature files
beside this one (read on 2026-10-28) they draw on. The default of every family is 022's; the record is
`genomes/confirm1_01.json`, which the defaults take once it holds across later batches. Evidence is a run in
the campaign ledger (`experiments/representation/speedrunning_plms/2026-10-28_afdb_v6_speedrun/runs.jsonl` in the
workspace), a screening effect (`search.py effects`), or a literature number, which transfers only as a prior.
One seed resolves about 0.0015 nats on `val_select`, so a single run inside that band is a tie.

## Outside the rules, or waiting on Logan's word

- A 3Di auxiliary target during training (`protein_pretraining.md` 3.2): the objective is fixed at MASK-only MLM,
  so this is not a candidate unless Logan opens the objective. Reported as the probe instead.
- Masking rate other than 15 percent, span or multi-rate masking: fixed by the rules.
- Sampled softmax, FP8 LM head, cross-entropy kernels, prefix-token prediction: built for a 50,000-token vocabulary
  or causal prediction; nothing to gain with 20 classes.
- Depth reduction: 36 blocks are fixed.
- Full Dust, the Cut chain, weight-space ES: out of a 10-minute budget on one GPU by the agents' own cost arithmetic.
- The 2.3x interpretability-inspired speedup post: its text was unreachable (HTTP 402); nothing to act on.
